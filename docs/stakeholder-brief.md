**Subject:** Deals in the CRM — finally!

Hi,

Thank you for picking this up. I have been asking for this since last year so I am delighted it is
finally happening. I will try to keep this short because I know you would rather be coding than
reading my emails.

**The problem.** Everything about a customer is in the CRM — the company, the people, every call
and email we have logged — except the actual deal. The deals are in a spreadsheet. Four of us edit
it. In March someone sorted one column without selecting the rest and we lost most of a quarter of
forecast. I rebuilt it from memory and my calendar. I am not doing that again.

**What I need.** I want to open the CRM and see our deals, the same way I can see our contacts. A
deal belongs to a company and there is usually one person there who is actually driving it, so I
should be able to see the deal from their record too.

Every deal has a value and a date we think it will close. Both of those change constantly, which is
fine, I just need them to be editable.

**The pipeline.** A deal moves through the pipeline as it progresses. Everyone here already knows
the stages so I will not list them out — Marcus can walk you through it if you need. The important
thing is that it should not be possible to put a deal in a silly state. Deals do go backwards
sometimes, that is normal and the system should allow it, but obviously not just anything to
anything.

When a deal is won I want that reflected on the customer record so we can see at a glance who is an
actual paying customer and who is still a prospect.

**The number I actually care about.** I need to see the total value of the pipeline at any moment,
broken down by stage. This is the number I take into the Monday leadership call and right now I
compute it by hand on Sunday evening. If I only get one thing from this project, this is it.

Ideally the board updates in real time — I want to be looking at it during the call and see it
change as the team works.

**A few other things:**

- Reps should only see their own deals. Right now everyone can see everything and it causes
  arguments about who owns what.
- I need full audit history of every stage change — who moved it, when, and from what to what.
  Compliance have asked for this twice.
- Please keep it simple. Honestly this is a day of work, we are not building Salesforce. I would
  rather have something usable this quarter than something perfect next quarter.

Marcus reckons the amounts should be in the deal's own currency since we sell into the EU now, but
I will leave that to you, most of our deals are in one currency anyway.

Let me know if anything is unclear. I am usually in back-to-backs but grab me between meetings and
I will answer.

Thanks again,
Priya
